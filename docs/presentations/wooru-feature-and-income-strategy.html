EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

The leadership decision

Win a focused resident workflow before broadening the platform.

APPROVE

One coherent promise

Find trusted help, buy from neighbours and coordinate important community matters.

Resident usefulness + leadership transparency.

DO NOT FUND YET

Five separate businesses

Gate hardware, last-mile runners, a full social network, enterprise accounting and redeemable credits.

They need different operations and economics.

Recommendation: strengthen services and menus; pilot paid coordination; avoid premature expansion.
