01 / PRODUCT STRATEGY · PROPOSAL, NOT A LIVE FEATURE

One community.
One concierge.
All its local sellers.

Not another WhatsApp Business inbox. Wooru is the community's discovery and order record—reachable by app or one opt-in chat. Existing groups remain human sharing channels.

One place to ask

“What vegetarian food is open on Sunday?”—across participating sellers in my society.

One trustworthy record

Menus, prices, capacity, order history and status live in Wooru, not in a thread or an AI model.

AI with a job

Help residents find things and help sellers draft them; never invent prices or take irreversible action without confirmation.

Concept only · Synthetic screens · No live WhatsApp or AI integration. Use ← → to navigate; Pause, Step and Replay control the scenes.

02 / DIFFERENTIATION

WhatsApp distributes the message. Wooru keeps the community record.

Typical seller-specific inbox or catalogProposed Wooru community layer
Helps a business manage its own chats, catalog and responses.Residents find participating sellers and structured offerings within their own society; sellers own their listings.
Can answer questions or share a product link; the conversation remains the main surface.A linked Wooru account and live records can ground cross-seller discovery, with the app and chat referring to the same order.
A message such as “two please” still needs order handling.For food, Wooru's server-checked pre-order tracks item, price, cutoff and capacity after explicit confirmation.
Community WhatsApp groups spread posts, but older offers get buried.Human-shared posts can become seller-approved, searchable Wooru listings; Wooru listings can be shared back as links, never silently synced.

For residents

Find community offerings in one place; food orders have a durable Wooru history instead of only a chat thread.

For sellers

Publish once in Wooru; choose when to share a listing into an existing group. No group listener or bulk auto-posting.

For leads

Moderate community listings with authorized tools and see aggregate activity, not private group or buyer conversations.

This compares common inbox/catalog workflows, not every competing product. Wooru membership is currently based on a join code; account linking does not prove real-world identity. Resale-product drafting and WhatsApp sharing here are proposals, not shipped features.

04 / EXISTING GROUPS · PROPOSED HUMAN SHARING

From group post to Wooru listing—by choice.

  • Priya submits her post. AI can draft, but only she can publish.
  • Priya chooses the group, previews the link and presses Send herself.

No existing-group sync. The live Wooru menu, not a stale preview, decides availability.

An authorized moderator can submit with seller permission; rejected or unclaimed drafts stay private. Unlinked visitors sign in before ordering. This sharing workflow is proposed, not shipped. The pilot remains food pre-orders; resale products come later.

05 / SELLER EXPERIENCE

The app remains the workshop. Chat is a shortcut.

  • AI helps draft. Priya reviews and publishes the menu in Wooru.
  • The same workspace receives Rahul’s confirmed order and manages capacity.

The app is the workshop; chat is an optional shortcut.

The app/PWA carries catalog, history, reviews, moderation and society administration. No WhatsApp or AI opt-in is required for core features. A seller-owned Business-number connection is optional and later, and never grants access to existing groups.

08 / PLATFORM REALITY AND SAFETY

Existing society groups are not an API data source.

Official routeWhat it supportsWhat it does not support
Business-app CoexistenceEligible seller's one-to-one app/API chat sync, subject to onboarding.Group chats are explicitly not synchronized.
Groups APIAPI-created, invite-only groups for Official Business Accounts, up to eight participants.No connector to an existing large society group; unavailable to Business-app phone numbers.

Supported human bridge

Seller or authorized moderator submits with permission. Seller approves or rejects the private draft; a person later previews a link, chooses a group and confirms Send.

Not automatic sync

No background reading, copying, or posting in existing groups. AI and messaging providers cannot bypass the platform boundary; unofficial browser bots are not a dependable foundation.

Trust before scale

Unclaimed/rejected drafts never publish. Detect duplicates; edit, close or expire outdated menus; show live availability after login. A group preview may remain stale. AI never invents an order.

Food orders still need linked identity, server-side price/cutoff/capacity checks and explicit buyer confirmation. Existing place_mcn_preorder relies on the signed-in user; a service-role key alone cannot impersonate the resident. Other business listings are contact-only today.

09 / ORDER OF OPERATIONS

First prove the community value. Then widen the channels.

Validate

Interview residents, sellers and leads. Confirm Wooru-owned number onboarding, opt-in, provider/AI contracts, data terms, human group-sharing friction and real costs.

Gate

Concierge pilot

One society, a few food sellers, one Wooru number. Link identity; search menus; confirm a food pre-order; check status. Structured fallback included.

Core

Test human sharing

Validate opt-in listing links and seller-approved drafts for existing groups. AI extraction can help but is optional; no group API sync.

Concept to validate

Expand only after proof

Try seller-number one-to-one Coexistence if eligible. Add resale products, service bookings or other commerce only after their Wooru lifecycle and safeguards exist.

Later

A good pilot is not “messages sent”

Measure linked residents, completed and repeat orders, discovery across sellers, seller time saved, abandoned orders, incorrect AI claims, opt-outs, support load and cost per completed order. Agree on baseline and thresholds before rollout.

10 / DECISION FOR STAKEHOLDERS

Fund a narrow pilot—not a generic chatbot program.

What to back

A Wooru-owned concierge for community-wide food discovery and one reliable pre-order flow. Treat deliberate group sharing as distribution, not the product or its database.

What to buy or verify

Wooru-owned business number and official messaging access, qualified data processor for optional AI, consent/legal review, and a real written provider quote.

What not to promise

Automatic reading or posting in existing society groups, universal product checkout, AI-generated confirmations without a transaction, or seller Coexistence before eligibility is checked.

Cost driverQuestion to answer in the pilot
Official one-to-one messagingMeta message categories, templates, number/provider charges and changing free tiers; what is the cost per completed order? A person sharing a link to a group is not an API-initiated group message.
AI assistanceHow often does it actually improve discovery or save seller time versus buttons and search? What does each assisted conversation cost?
Engineering and trustIdentity, retries, seller approval, duplicate drafts, moderation, data processing, support and recovery; which expenses recur as sellers grow?
