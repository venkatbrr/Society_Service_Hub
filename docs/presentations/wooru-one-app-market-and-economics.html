07Product · proposed AI

Answers from the society’s own documents

The committee uploads bye-laws, AGM minutes and circulars once. Residents ask in their own words. Every answer shows where it came from. Proposed, not built.

How it works
1UploadA lead adds a PDF and marks it public or committee-only.
2ReadWooru indexes passages and keeps each page reference.
3AskResidents ask in plain language; only allowed files are searched.
4AnswerReplies cite pages. If nothing fits, Wooru says so.

Safeguards

08Product · sequencing

Build in stages, and check what works at each step

Nowexisting features

Make what exists reliable

  • Test login, recovery and alert delivery
  • Make providers and menus easier to use
  • Make the funds ledger easier for residents to understand

What to checkPilot residents sign in and get alerts without calling for help.

Nextpilot, 3–5 societies

Notices and issue follow‑through

  • Posts/events by audience; lead-only notices
  • Issues with a named owner and status
  • Two-way WhatsApp on one Wooru number
  • Clear privacy and contact choices

What to checkFewer repeat follow-ups on similar issues (working target 25%).

Laterwith partners

Connected operations

  • Facility booking and polls
  • Society billing and payment status
  • Visitor access with gate partners

What to checkPilot societies agree to pay for coordination.

Built by our existing team on our existing app and infrastructure. Stages after “Now” are proposals; scope and timing depend on demand, costs and integrations.
