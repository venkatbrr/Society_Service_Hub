What Wooru runs

One app for the everyday work of community life.

Trusted providers

Plumbers, electricians, maids, tutors and dozens more categories — rated by residents in your own society, with private notes and a fraud check on every new listing.

Food drops

Pre-order from home kitchens before the cutoff, with live counts and a delivery roster for hosts.

Community funds

Transparent ledgers for maintenance and festival collections — income against expense, in full view.

My Community Network

Resident-run businesses, carpooling, and a parent corner — the day-to-day coordination of your society, brought into one tab.

Service reminders, kept private

Water purifier, AC service, gas refill, pest control — set a schedule once and Wooru nudges you before it falls due. Your reminders are yours alone: no neighbour, committee member or admin ever sees them.

Shared service visits

Schedule a provider visit and let neighbours join — split the cost and the coordination.

Community events

Cultural, sports and festival events posted by coordinators — venue, timing and contacts in one card.

Emergency SOS

An emergency contact directory plus an opt-in blood-donor registry, filterable by group.

Installs like an app

Add Wooru to your home screen straight from the browser — no app store download needed.

Residents directory

Verified neighbours grouped by block or tower, with contact details scoped to your role.

Realtime notifications

Visit updates, fund approvals and reminders arrive the moment they happen.

Food drops

The home kitchen next door, finally organised.

A resident chef opens a drop, sets a fulfilment window and a cutoff, and neighbours pre-order before it closes. No delivery apps, no commissions — just food made a few doors away.

For residents

·Fresh, home-cooked food from people you actually know
·Reserve before the cutoff and edit or cancel while a drop is open
·Pay the chef directly — no markup, no delivery fees

For home businesses

·Take orders from a captive, trusting audience in your own society
·Set per-item limits and a cutoff, then cook to the exact count
·A delivery roster does the coordination — no group-chat chaos
Transparency, by default

The committee finally has nothing left to explain.

Roles that fit large societies
